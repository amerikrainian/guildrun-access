# The Run

## How It Is Laid Out

New Run takes you to the difficulty screen: the tiers, the modifiers, your streak, and the leaderboard. The eighth tier, the Red Rift, unlocks once you beat a high enough difficulty; it adds the Rift Seal and six missions (Ctrl+M reads them from anywhere).

A run is two acts of six floors each. Every floor is a fight; between fights you get a crossroads (pick a path) or an event, and shops after results. The first act ends on its act boss, the second on the final boss. Ctrl+B names the boss of the act you are in.

## The Hero Picker

The run opens on a hero picker. Each offer is a hero card: name, classes, rank, stats, abilities, and on the first picker a bundled relic that comes with the hero. The hero buffer holds the whole card; Alt+R reads the relic as its tooltip shows it, and the relics buffer has it on top.

With a Boss Token in your profile, past the tutorial, the picker offers a reroll, listed after the heroes with your token count.

## The Run Screen

While placing and while fighting, the run screen is a set of control groups that Tab cycles through:

- **Fight**: the button that starts the battle, while placing. Alt+F presses it from anywhere.
- **Board**: the hex grid. See [The Board And Combat](combat.md).
- **Party**: a hero per row: the hero, then its item slots ("slot 1, empty"). Up and Down keep the column, so you can walk one slot down the whole party. The reserve follows the party.
- **Inventory**: the reserve's items and the run's relics as two columns. Up and Down within one, Left and Right across.
- **Info**: gold, shards, difficulty, the battle timer.
- **Map**: the current act's floors, the current one marked, the act boss, and the final boss.
- **Sidebar**: the game's information panel: an inspected card, the damage tracker, the Red Rift challenge.
- **Battle events**: the last fight's log, newest first, once a fight has shown something.
- **Battle speed**: auto and the fixed speeds.
- **Menu**: the Heroes panel, Feedback, Settings, and Show while a rank-up choice is hidden.

Alt+B, Alt+T, and Alt+I jump to the board, the party, and the items from anywhere on the screen.

## Heroes

Enter on a hero (a party slot, a reserve slot, or its board cell) opens its menu:

- **Inspect**: shows its card in the sidebar and lands you on it.
- **Move**: picks the hero up; Enter on a board cell puts it there, swapping with whoever stands on it. Escape cancels.
- **Place on board** (from the reserve) and **Move to reserve**.
- **Unequip** for each item it wears.
- **Sell**, while the shop is up, with the price.

Backspace on a hero opens it in the compendium.

## Items

Enter on an item in the reserve opens "Equip item to": a row per hero, a choice per slot, the item already there named. Equipping lands focus on the slot the item went into.

Enter on an item a hero wears offers moving it to another of that hero's slots (swapping with what is there), unequipping it, and selling it in the shop.

Alt+digit names what the focused hero holds in that slot; add Shift for the item's tooltip.
