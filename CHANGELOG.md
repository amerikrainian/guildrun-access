# Changelog

## V0.0.1

- First release: the main menu, its dialogs and settings, the run-start screen, the hero picker, and the whole run read by keyboard: the placement grid with keyboard moves, the party and reserve with equip and unequip menus, items, relics, the act map, the sidebar, battle speed and the menu.
- Fights: every unit on the battlefield with its live health, shield and mana, re-read while you rest on it; the battle events log as its own stop; nothing spoken over your navigation as the fight runs.
- Battle results, the shop, crossroads, events, rank-up pickers, the Heroes panel, the end screen and progression, the compendium, comics, and tooltips through the game's own tooltip pipeline.
- Difficulty tiers read with the rank their icon shows ("LETHAL C"), and Space on an unlocked tier reads what it means.
- A mod menu (Ctrl+Shift+M) for the mod's settings and its key help.
