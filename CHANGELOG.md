# Changelog

## V0.0.2

- The relic reward picker ("Choose your reward" after a challenge fight, and any pending relic
  choice) now reads: each relic on offer with its name, rarity and effect, the full tooltip in the
  control buffer, then Skip Reward and Back where the game shows them. Enter picks, Escape presses
  Back. It was silent before.
- Enemies the game's own data leaves nameless (295 of its 644 enemy entries, the scaled variants of a
  creature) are named after their named sibling ("Forest Golem", "Mushroom Tank") on the board, in the
  enemies buffer, on the sidebar card, in the battle events and in the result's versus line, where
  before only their health and stats read.
- The mod now checks for updates after loading and says "update X available" when a newer release
  exists. Up to date, it keeps the news to itself.
